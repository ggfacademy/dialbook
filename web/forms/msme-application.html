How to use this form

  1. Fill in your details below. Everything stays on your phone – nothing is sent to anyone.
  2. Tap Print / Save as PDF at the bottom. Choose your printer, or Save as PDF to print it at a shop.
  3. Sign the printed form, paste your passport photo (if you did not add it here) and bring it on the first day of class with your documents.

Prefer to write by hand? Download the blank form (PDF).
