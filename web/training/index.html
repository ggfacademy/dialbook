Your daily work in Dialbook

Sign in, call your leads, log every call, keep your follow-ups and reply on WhatsApp.

Tap a part to jump to it. The video shows sample leads, not real customers. Download the video to share it on WhatsApp.

Your daily routine

  1. Morning: open Follow-ups and call everything overdue and due today.
  2. Then Calling mode → New leads, never called.
  3. After every call, pick the outcome and set the next follow-up. Never leave a call without an outcome.
  4. Reply to WhatsApp chats that show a green number.
  5. When someone joins, pick Converted / Sale, so it counts in your monthly report.

1. Sign in

  1. Open ggfacademy.github.io/dialbook in Chrome (phone or computer).
  2. Type your email and password and tap Sign in.
  3. First time? Tap Create account, then ask your admin to approve you in Team. Until then you see “Waiting for approval”.

2. Your dashboard

  1. Shows your calls, connected calls, talk time, interested and converted leads.
  2. A red “Follow-ups due” number means callbacks are overdue: call them first.
  3. Next follow-ups lists who to call next, with Call and Open buttons.

3. Your leads

  1. Leads shows only the leads assigned to you. New Facebook and WhatsApp leads arrive on their own.
  2. Search by name, phone or city. Filter by stage, campaign, language, or Follow-up due today.
  3. Tap a lead to open it. You can change its Stage, Priority and Language, read notes and past calls, and tap Call or WhatsApp / SMS.

4. Add a new lead

  1. For walk-ins, referrals or incoming calls: Leads → Add lead.
  2. Fill Name and Phone, pick the Source (Walk-in, Referral…), and the program under Campaign if you know it.
  3. Tap Add lead. It is assigned to you. If the number already exists, the CRM warns you.

5. Calling mode: call, log, next

  1. Open Calling mode. Pick who to call: New leads, never called, Follow-ups due today, or all open leads. Tap Start calling.
  2. You see one lead at a time, with the program’s call script and the last calls.
  3. Tap Call now. After the call pick the outcome (Interested, Call back later, Did not pick…), set the next follow-up and write a short note.
  4. Tap Save and next lead. Use Skip if you cannot call this person now.

6. Follow-ups

  1. Follow-ups shows the callbacks you promised: Overdue (red), Today, then the next 7 days.
  2. Call all due now starts Calling mode with every follow-up that is due.

7. WhatsApp

  1. WhatsApp lists your chats. A green number means unread replies.
  2. Open a chat, type, and tap Send. Messages go from the company WhatsApp number.
  3. Normal messages work within 24 hours of the lead’s last message. To start a chat, or after 24 hours, tap Template, check the values (the name is filled in) and tap Send template.

8. The Dialbook phone app

  1. Install it once from ggfacademy.github.io/dialbook/download/dialbook.apk (Android). Allow installing from Chrome if asked.
  2. Paste the connection code your admin sends you → Connect → sign in with the same email and password.
  3. Allow every permission, and turn on call recording in your phone’s own dialer settings.
  4. My leads → tap Call. When you hang up, a notification asks How did the call go? Pick the outcome, the follow-up and a note → Save outcome.
  5. Missed one? It waits under To log. Status shows the app is connected; tap Check for new calls now if a call is missing.

Need help?

Can’t sign in, see no leads, or the app is not logging calls? Ask your admin. They can check Team and Settings in the CRM.
