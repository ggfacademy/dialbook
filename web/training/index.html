Telecaller daily routine

  1. Morning: Follow-ups – call everything overdue and due today.
  2. Then Calling mode → New leads, never called, hot leads first.
  3. After every call: outcome and next follow-up, and the reason when someone is not interested.
  4. Reply to WhatsApp chats with a green number; send the brochure, application form and certificate sample.
  5. Mark Converted / Sale when someone pays.

Admin daily checklist

  1. Morning: Dashboard – have callers started? Team – approve new people.
  2. Leads – share out unassigned leads; check never-called hot leads.
  3. AI calling – start a list for leads not reached (test with 1 first).
  4. Evening: Reports – speed to lead, conversions and lost reasons.
  5. Weekly: update course dates and venues in Campaigns.
