Privacy Policy

GGF Leads CRM (Dialbook) · Global Gold Foundation Pvt Ltd · Last updated 30 September 2026

Who we are

GGF Leads CRM is the internal customer-relationship tool of Global Gold Foundation Pvt Ltd. Our staff use it to contact people who asked us for information about our courses and services.

What we collect

  • Details you submit in our Facebook, Instagram or Google lead forms, on our website or in other enquiry forms, such as your name, phone number, email, city, preferred language and your answers to the form's questions.
  • A record of our calls and WhatsApp messages with you, including call outcomes, notes and, where the law allows, call recordings.

How we use it

  • To answer your enquiry and follow up by phone, SMS or WhatsApp.
  • To assign your enquiry to a team member who speaks your language.
  • To keep records of our service and improve it.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Who processes it

Your data is stored on Supabase (database hosting). Messages and calls are sent through our messaging and calling providers, such as Meta (WhatsApp) and Interakt. These providers process data only to deliver our service.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry records for as long as needed to serve you and to meet legal and accounting duties, and then delete them.

Your choices and data deletion

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your data, or to stop contacting you. Reply "STOP" on WhatsApp, tell the team member who calls you, or contact Global Gold Foundation Pvt Ltd through the Facebook Page where you submitted the form. We will delete your records within 30 days of your request.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
